# Notice — copyright and licensing

## Current position: all rights reserved

**No license is granted for this specification.** In the absence of a license, the default under copyright law is that no permission is given to copy, modify, redistribute, or create derivative works.

This is stated explicitly because silence is easily misread. A public repository with no `LICENSE` file is sometimes assumed to be open source; it is not. It is also sometimes confused with *The Unlicense*, a public-domain dedication that means very nearly the opposite of the position stated here.

## Why it is unresolved

The licensing of a protocol specification is not the same decision as the licensing of software, and getting it wrong is difficult to reverse. Two considerations are still open:

- **The standards path.** This specification is being prepared for submission as an Internet-Draft. That path carries its own intellectual-property requirements, including grants that would be incompatible with some licenses chosen now. Deciding prematurely could foreclose it.
- **The specification and the implementation want different terms.** A specification's goal is that anyone may implement it while the standard itself does not fragment. An implementation's goal is different. They are likely to be licensed differently, and the specification's terms should be settled deliberately rather than inherited from the code.

Licensing will be resolved before any public release, and no later than submission of an Internet-Draft.

## What this means in practice

**Reading and evaluating.** Reading this specification, forming a view on it, discussing it publicly, and reporting defects require no license and are welcome. Ideas, protocols, and methods are not themselves subject to copyright — this notice concerns the *text* of these documents.

**Implementing.** No permission is granted to reproduce or create derivative works of this text. If you intend to build an implementation and want certainty about your position, ask before starting rather than after.

**Redistributing.** Do not copy these documents elsewhere, in whole or in part, while this position stands.

**Contributing.** Contributions are not being accepted yet. Accepting them before licensing is settled would create rights held by others across a text whose terms are undecided, which is precisely the situation this notice exists to avoid.
